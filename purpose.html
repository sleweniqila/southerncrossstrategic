Vision & Mission

Corporate Vision

“To become the South Pacific’s most trusted and respected strategic holdings company, delivering integrated business solutions through professional leadership, innovation, operational excellence, and sustainable partnerships that create lasting value for our shareholders, clients, employees, communities, and future generations.”

The Vision sets the position the Group intends to hold across Papua New Guinea, Fiji and the wider South Pacific, and the standard every subsidiary is measured against.

Corporate Mission

“To provide strategic leadership and corporate governance through a professionally managed holding company that empowers its subsidiaries to deliver safe, innovative, efficient, and sustainable solutions while creating long-term value for our clients, employees, shareholders, landowners, communities, and strategic partners throughout Papua New Guinea and the South Pacific.”

Leading with integrity, delivering with excellence — the Mission is the operational foundation that aligns every subsidiary, department and employee to one common purpose.

Core values

Our values define our culture. Our culture shapes our future.

Eight values give the Holding Company and every subsidiary one ethical foundation, regardless of business function or operating location.

Integrity

Doing what is right when it is difficult, and when no one is checking.

Professionalism

Competent, disciplined conduct on every site and in every dealing.

Accountability

Clear ownership of decisions, budgets and outcomes at every level.

Safety

No task is so urgent that it cannot be done safely. People come home.

Respect

For landowners, communities, cultures and every member of the workforce.

Partnership

Long-term relationships with government, clients, landowners and suppliers.

Innovation

Better systems, better technology, better ways of delivering the work.

Sustainability

Growth that holds — financially, environmentally and socially.
