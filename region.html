Where we operate

One corridor, two countries, one chain of command.

The Group is registered and recruits in Fiji, and delivers into project areas in Papua New Guinea — with the Holding Company holding governance across both ends of that corridor.

That geography is the reason the Group is structured as it is. Workforce, payroll, compliance and welfare stay under Fiji governance; security, engineering and logistics delivery sit at the project end. One framework spans both, so a decision taken in Suva is auditable on site.
