The Group

Four subsidiaries. One governance framework.

Each subsidiary is a centre of excellence in its own field. Together they form an integrated service platform capable of supporting major public and private sector projects.

Integrated Security Solutions

Protective security, security risk management, emergency response, executive protection, K9 services and specialised security operations.

The security subsidiary carries the Group’s frontline responsibility: protecting personnel, assets and operations across project corridors where terrain, community relationships and access all shape the risk picture. Operations are governed by written policy and field-level standard operating procedures, with a 24/7 operations room providing command, communications, movement control and incident escalation.

  • Risk & threat management
  • Security operations & static guarding
  • Training & capacity building
  • Close protection services

Infrastructure & Engineering

Engineering, infrastructure development, technical support, construction management and maintenance services.

The engineering subsidiary delivers and maintains the physical infrastructure projects depend on — camps, access routes, site facilities and technical support — with trade capability across electrical, carpentry, plumbing and mechanical disciplines, and competency, certification and PPE standards defined at group level.

Construction managementSite facilitiesMaintenanceTechnical supportTrade services
  • Architectural design
  • Civil & structural engineering
  • Project management
  • Infrastructure advisory

Logistics & Procurement

Supply chain management, procurement, transportation, fleet management, warehousing and operational logistics.

Remote operations succeed or fail on supply. This subsidiary manages the movement of people, equipment and stores by road, river, coast and air, under journey management and convoy procedures, with procurement controls and stores discipline that keep spend accountable and traceable.

  • Supply chain management
  • Freight & transport
  • Procurement services
  • Warehousing & distribution

Corporate Support Services

Finance, Human Resources, ICT, Legal, Compliance, Administration, Corporate Strategy and Business Development.

The corporate subsidiary holds the systems that let the others operate: recruitment and workforce development, payroll and financial control, ICT and cybersecurity, records and compliance, and the strategy function that maintains this plan and reports performance to the Board.
