Who we are

A holding company built for governance first.

Southern Cross Strategic Holdings Pte Limited (SCSH) was established to provide executive leadership, corporate governance, strategic oversight and sustainable business development across a diversified group of specialised operating subsidiaries.

The structure is deliberate. The Holding Company carries governance, financial oversight, enterprise risk management and performance monitoring, so each subsidiary can concentrate on its own field of expertise while staying aligned to one strategic direction. For a client, that means several professional services accessible through a single coordinated corporate structure.

The Group is positioned to support major infrastructure projects, energy and resource developments, government programmes, commercial and industrial operations, security and risk management, supply chain and logistics, and corporate advisory work throughout Papua New Guinea, Fiji and the South Pacific.

Where we operate

One corridor, two countries, one chain of command.

The Group is registered and recruits in Fiji, and delivers into project areas in Papua New Guinea — with the Holding Company holding governance across both ends of that corridor.

That geography is the reason the Group is structured as it is. Workforce, payroll, compliance and welfare stay under Fiji governance; security, engineering and logistics delivery sit at the project end. One framework spans both, so a decision taken in Suva is auditable on site.

Implementation roadmap

Compliance before operations.

The Group implements its plan in five sequenced phases. Nothing begins before the approvals that authorise it — that principle governs the entire roadmap.

Phase 01

Corporate establishment & government approval

Finalise governance systems and policies, obtain all required licences and approvals from the Government of Fiji, strengthen internal management systems.

Outcome — SCSH fully authorised to commence operations.

Phase 02

Strategic partnership formalisation

Execute the proposed Memorandum of Agreement and Joint Venture Agreement, confirm operational coordination arrangements, finalise implementation planning.

Outcome — strategic partnership formally established.

Phase 03

Operational readiness

Recruit key personnel, complete induction and training, establish subsidiary operating procedures, confirm budgets and reporting systems, test readiness.

Outcome — Holding Company and all four subsidiaries prepared to operate.

Phase 04

Recruitment operations

Commence lawful recruitment, verify candidate qualifications, complete employment documentation, deliver pre-departure orientation, coordinate deployment.

Outcome — professional and compliant recruitment operations.

Phase 05

Continuous improvement

Monitor performance, conduct internal audits, review strategic objectives and subsidiary performance against approved budgets, strengthen engagement.

Outcome — long-term sustainability and continuous improvement.

Governance & assurance

How the Group holds itself to account.

The Holding Company provides strategic oversight while ensuring each subsidiary operates efficiently, responsibly and in accordance with Group policies and applicable legislation.

Governance is documented, not assumed: board and executive responsibilities are defined, policies are issued at group level with matching standard operating procedures, and performance is reviewed on a fixed schedule against approved budgets and KPIs.

Corporate governance

Board oversight, defined executive authority, group policy framework and documented delegation.

Enterprise risk management

Risk identification, assessment and treatment with risk-based decision-making across all operations.

Health, safety, environment & social

HSES governance, hierarchy of control, incident reporting and investigation, and a performance dashboard.

Audit, quality & compliance

Internal audit, quality assurance and compliance functions with separate policies and procedures.

ICT & cybersecurity

Digital transformation, information security controls and operational technology for field operations.

Financial control

Budget governance, monitoring and variance escalation, with subsidiary accountability for spend.
